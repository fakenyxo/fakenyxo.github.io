About Us

About Mohd Kaif Khan

Who I am

I'm Mohd Kaif Khan, an HVAC design engineer and independent freelance consultant based in New Delhi, India, serving clients across the NCR region (Delhi, Gurgaon, Noida).

What I do

I provide HVAC design services for residential, commercial and hospital projects:

  • Concept Design — heat load summary and equipment selection
  • Drawings Design — HVAC layout drawing
  • Detailed Design — single-line HVAC scheme, heat load calculations, and equipment selection with drawings
  • Design + Site Visit — Detailed Design plus 2 site visits

All deliverables are digital (PDF drawings and documents).

How I work

Pricing is transparent and based on built-up area. Fees are paid in clear stages tied to deliverables, each scope includes a stated number of revisions, and the final fee is confirmed in writing before work begins. See Services & Pricing for rates.
