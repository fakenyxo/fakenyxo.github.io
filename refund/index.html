Refund & Cancellation Policy

Version 1.0 · Last updated 7 October 2026. This policy applies to HVAC design services by Mohd Kaif Khan and forms part of the Terms & Conditions.

1. Nature of services

Services are digital professional services delivered in stages, and payment is made in stages as shown in your quote. No physical goods are sold.

2. Cancellation before work starts

If you cancel before work begins, the advance is refunded in full, less any bank or payment-gateway charges.

3. Cancellation after work starts

Once work has started, the advance and any stage payments already made are non-refundable. Work in progress on the current stage is charged proportionately, and stages not yet started are not payable.

4. If I cannot complete the work

If I am unable to complete the agreed work, fees paid for stages that were not performed are refunded.

5. Duplicate or failed payments

If you were charged twice, or the amount was debited but not confirmed, send your payment proof and quote reference through Support. A duplicate payment is refunded in full. Failed transactions are normally reversed by your bank according to its own timelines.

6. How to request a refund or cancellation

Send a request through Support or email mohdkaifkhan.w@gmail.com with your name, quote reference and payment reference. I reply within 24 hours.

7. Refund timeline and method

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 7 working days. The time taken for the amount to reach your account depends on your bank or payment provider.

8. Revisions and quality concerns

Dissatisfaction with a revision count is not a ground for refund; additional revisions are handled as described in the Terms. If you find an error in a deliverable, notify me within 30 days of delivery and I will correct it at no extra charge.
