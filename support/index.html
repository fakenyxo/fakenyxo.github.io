Support

How can I help you?

Payment confirmation, revisions, project updates or invoices — find a quick answer below or send a support request. I respond within 24 hours.

Common questions

How do I pay?

On the Hire Me page, choose your building type, built-up area and scope, fill in your details and accept the terms, then pay the advance by UPI (button or QR). After paying, send the payment screenshot or UPI reference with your quote reference (it starts with KK-).

I've paid but haven't had confirmation

Send your quote reference and the UPI reference or screenshot using the form. Work begins once the advance is confirmed as received in my account.

How many revisions are included?

Concept Design: 2. Drawings Design: 2. Detailed Design: 5. Design + Site Visit: 5. Additional revisions beyond those included may be charged separately, and the charge is confirmed before that work starts.

When do I receive final files?

Each stage deliverable may first be shared in review form. The final issued version is released once that stage's payment is received. See the Terms & Conditions for details.

Can I get a site visit?

Design + Site Visit includes 2 visits within NCR. For Concept or Drawings Design, request a site visit quote on the Hire Me page or in the form here.

What about cancellation or refunds?

Cancellation and refunds follow section 11 of the Terms & Conditions. Write to me with your quote reference and I'll reply with next steps.

How is my information used?

See the Privacy Policy. To access, correct or delete your information, send a support request or email me.

A quote reference is required for payment, additional revision, site visit, invoice and refund requests. You will find it on the Hire Me page after you request a quote, and in your confirmation message.
