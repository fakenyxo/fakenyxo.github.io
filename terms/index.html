Version 1.0 · Last updated 7 October 2026. These terms are between Mohd Kaif Khan, an independent freelance HVAC designer ("Designer"), and the person or business named in the quote ("Client").

1. Agreement

Ticking "I agree", paying any amount, or sending a quote request by WhatsApp/email means the Client accepts these terms and the quote (building type, built-up area, scope, fee and payment stages). Electronic acceptance is intended to be binding. Work begins only after the advance is received. Refunds are governed by the Refund & Cancellation Policy, and delivery by the Service Delivery Policy; both form part of this agreement.

2. Scope of work

Services are HVAC design only, limited to the scope in the quote:
• Concept Design — heat load summary and equipment selection.
• Drawings Design — HVAC layout drawing.
• Detailed Design — single-line HVAC scheme drawing, heat load calculations, and equipment selection with drawings.
• Design + Site Visit — everything in Detailed Design plus 2 site visits.
Not included unless agreed in writing: electrical, plumbing, fire fighting, structural or civil design, other trades, statutory approvals or NOCs, procurement, supervision, testing and commissioning. Extra work is quoted separately.

3. Fees & payment

The fee is estimated from the building type and built-up area the Client provides. If the actual area, usage or building type differs, or the drawings show a different scope, the fee is adjusted and confirmed in writing. Payment stages: Concept Design and Drawings Design — 50% advance, 50% on submission of the final deliverable. Detailed Design and Design + Site Visit — 20% advance; 30% on submission of the single-line HVAC scheme drawing; 40% on submission of heat load calculations/equipment with drawings; 10% after final approval of drawings and calculations. Each stage payment is due within 7 days of submission. The next stage may be paused until payment is received. A minimum project fee of ₹2,000 applies to residential and commercial projects. GST and other taxes are extra if applicable. Payment is by UPI or the payment link shared; any bank/gateway charges are borne by the payer. Payment queries must be raised in writing within 7 days.

4. Deliverables, approval & release

Each stage deliverable may first be shared in review form. The final issued version of a stage, and any editable files (e.g. CAD, only if agreed in writing), are released once that stage's payment, and for editable files all payments, is received. If the Client gives no written feedback within 10 days of submission (and a reminder), the stage is treated as accepted and its payment falls due.

5. Revisions

Included revisions: Concept Design — 2; Drawings Design — 2; Detailed Design — 5; Design + Site Visit — 5. A revision is one consolidated set of comments on a submitted deliverable within the original design basis. Changes to the design basis (new layout or architectural changes, load or usage changes, change of equipment type or make, new areas) are new work and quoted separately. Additional revisions beyond those included may be charged separately, with the charge confirmed before that work starts. Unused revisions lapse when the project closes.

6. Client responsibilities

The Client will provide accurate and complete inputs (architectural drawings including floor plans, sections and elevations; building orientation; building envelope data such as SHGC and U-values; project location; and requirements), a single point of contact, and timely feedback. The Client is responsible for verifying dimensions and site conditions, coordinating with other trades, and obtaining statutory approvals. The Designer is not responsible for errors, delays or extra work caused by incorrect, late or changed inputs. If the project is inactive for over 30 days because of the Client, the Designer may close the file; completed stages remain payable and restarting may be re-quoted.

7. Timelines

Timelines are good-faith estimates that start after the advance and required inputs are received. They extend for Client delays, scope changes and events beyond the Designer's control.

8. Site visits

Design + Site Visit includes 2 site visits within the NCR region for design coordination and verification. For other scopes, a site visit can be requested and is quoted separately; requesting a quote creates no obligation on either side. Additional visits and travel outside NCR are charged separately. Visits are not construction supervision or quality inspection of the contractor's work.

They may not be reused for other projects or sites, or resold, without the Designer's written consent. The Client keeps ownership of materials it supplies and confirms it has the right to share them. Until full payment, deliverables are for review only and not to be used for construction or procurement. After work starts, the advance and stages already invoiced are non-refundable, and work in progress on the current stage is charged proportionately.
