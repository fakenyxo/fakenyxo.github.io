Independent engineering support at the decision points that matter

Many HVAC problems begin long before installation: in the load assumptions, equipment selection, duct routes and specifications written into the design. The right checks at these stages help the project team make informed decisions before they become expensive site changes.

Design deliverables can include stated assumptions, calculation summaries, coordinated drawings and technical schedules, giving your architect, contractor and MEP team a clear basis for review.

The five services

What an HVAC engagement covers

Each service can be taken independently or combined into a coordinated design package, depending on the stage and requirements of your project.

01Design

HVAC design consultancy

HVAC system planning and design support from concept through detailed drawings. Review the building requirements, compare suitable system options and develop a practical design basis for the project.

  • ✓HVAC system concept and design basis
  • ✓Equipment selection and capacity checks
  • ✓HVAC layouts, schematics and sections
  • ✓Design coordination with other MEP services
02Engineering

Load calculations & sizing

Room-by-room cooling load calculations and equipment sizing based on architectural drawings, occupancy, orientation, envelope properties and internal loads. Inputs and assumptions are kept clear for review.

  • ✓Cooling load calculations using Carrier HAP
  • ✓Equipment capacity selection with assumptions stated
  • ✓Fresh-air and ventilation load considerations
  • ✓Calculation summaries for design review
03Air distribution

Ventilation & duct design

Fresh-air requirements, exhaust ventilation and air distribution planning developed around room use, occupancy and project criteria, with coordinated layouts for practical installation.

  • ✓Fresh-air and exhaust air calculations
  • ✓Duct sizing and routing layouts
  • ✓Ventilation schedules and design notes
  • ✓Coordination with architectural and MEP drawings
04Compliance

Standards & design documentation

Design criteria and documentation can be checked against the standards and codes applicable to the project. Requirements are identified clearly so the project team can review and close design gaps.

  • ✓Ventilation and filtration design criteria
  • ✓ASHRAE and ISHRAE reference checks where applicable
  • ✓ECBC considerations for relevant projects
  • ✓Design notes, schedules and calculation records
05Documentation

Tender & vendor support

Support for HVAC tender packages and technical coordination, helping project teams communicate the intended scope and compare equipment submissions on a like-for-like basis.

  • ✓Technical specifications and scope documents
  • ✓Bill of quantities review and preparation
  • ✓Vendor offer comparison on technical details
  • ✓Drawing and submittal coordination

Working references

Recognised standards. Clear design records.

Project criteria are selected according to building use, local requirements and the agreed scope of work.

ISHRAE guidelines

Indian HVAC and indoor environmental guidance considered where relevant to the building type and design scope.

ASHRAE standards

Applicable ventilation, comfort and energy references used as design inputs where specified by the project.

ECBC requirements

Energy conservation provisions considered for projects where ECBC applicability is established.

Design handover

Calculation summaries, schedules, assumptions and coordinated drawings organised for project review.
