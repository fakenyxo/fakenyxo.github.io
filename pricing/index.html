Services & Pricing

HVAC design fees are charged per square foot of built-up area. All prices are in Indian Rupees (INR).

Rates (₹ per sq ft)

Building typeConcept DesignDrawings DesignDetailed DesignDesign + Site Visit
Residential₹3₹3₹6₹8
Commercial₹5₹5₹10₹12
Hospital₹6₹6₹12₹15

Minimum project fee: ₹2,000 for residential and commercial projects. GST and other taxes are extra if applicable. The final fee is confirmed in writing after I review your drawings and requirements.

What each service includes

  • Concept Design — heat load summary and equipment selection · 2 revisions
  • Drawings Design — HVAC layout drawing · 2 revisions
  • Detailed Design — single-line HVAC scheme drawing, heat load calculations, equipment selection with drawings · 5 revisions
  • Design + Site Visit — everything in Detailed Design plus 2 site visits within NCR · 5 revisions

Additional revisions beyond those included may be charged separately. Site visits for Concept and Drawings Design are quoted on request.

Payment stages

  • Concept Design and Drawings Design: 50% advance, 50% on submission of the deliverable
  • Detailed Design and Design + Site Visit: 20% advance, 30% after single-line HVAC scheme drawing submission, 40% after heat load calculations/equipment with drawings submission, 10% after final approval

See the Refund & Cancellation Policy and Service Delivery Policy for details.
