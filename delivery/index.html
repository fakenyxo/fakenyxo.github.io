Service Delivery Policy

Version 1.0 · Last updated 7 October 2026. This policy applies to HVAC design services by Mohd Kaif Khan and forms part of the Terms & Conditions.

1. Nature of delivery

All services are digital. No physical goods are shipped, so there are no shipping charges.

2. How deliverables are sent

Drawings and documents are delivered as PDF files by email, WhatsApp or a download link, to the contact details you provided.

3. Delivery by stage

  • Concept Design: load summary and equipment selection
  • Drawings Design: HVAC layout drawing
  • Detailed Design and Design + Site Visit: single-line HVAC scheme drawing; heat load calculations and equipment with drawings; final approved set

4. Timelines

Timelines are estimates agreed with you and start after the advance and required inputs (architectural drawings with floor plans, sections and elevations; building orientation; SHGC and U-values; project location; and requirements) are received. Client delays, scope changes and events beyond my control extend them accordingly.

5. Review and release

Each stage deliverable may first be shared in review form. The final issued version is released once that stage's payment is received, as described in the Terms.

6. Site visits

Design + Site Visit includes 2 site visits within NCR, scheduled with you in advance. For other services, a site visit is quoted separately on request.

7. Not received something?

If you have not received a deliverable, contact Support with your quote reference and I will resend it.
